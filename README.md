# FIS Global (fis)
FIS (Fidelity National Information Services) is a global provider of financial services technology with solutions in retail and institutional banking, payments, asset and wealth management, risk and compliance, and outsourcing. APIs are surfaced through the FIS CodeConnect marketplace, which connects financial institutions, fintechs, and enterprises to FIS banking and payment infrastructure across core banking, payments, and wealth management.
